Clues from the past

Fossils: Earth's Time Capsules

Fossils preserve remains or traces of ancient life. Scientists study them to learn about extinct species and long-lost environments.

  • Fossil bones: reveal clues about body structure.
  • Footprints: provide evidence of movement.
  • Fossil plants: help reconstruct ancient habitats.
  • New discoveries: improve our understanding of history.

A journey through deep time

The Mesozoic Era

The Mesozoic Era is divided into three periods. The dates below are approximate.

Triassic Period · 252–201 million years ago

Early dinosaurs appeared during the Triassic, alongside many other animal groups.

Jurassic Period · 201–145 million years ago

Dinosaurs diversified, and some of the largest land animals lived during this time.

Cretaceous Period · 145–66 million years ago

Many well-known dinosaurs lived during this period before the end-Cretaceous extinction.

About 66 million years ago

A large asteroid impact contributed to the extinction of all non-avian dinosaurs. Birds are living avian dinosaurs.
