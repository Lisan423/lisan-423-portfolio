A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

The Prehistoric Timeline

Explore the three periods of the Mesozoic Era.

Triassic Period · About 252–201 million years ago

Early dinosaurs appeared during the Triassic. The continents were arranged very differently from today.

Jurassic Period · About 201–145 million years ago

Many dinosaurs diversified. Giant long-necked sauropods and numerous other dinosaur groups flourished.

Cretaceous Period · About 145–66 million years ago

Dinosaurs such as Tyrannosaurus and Triceratops lived during this period, which ended with a mass extinction.

Today · The Age of Living Birds

Birds are the surviving dinosaur lineage. Palaeontologists continue discovering new evidence about their ancestors.
